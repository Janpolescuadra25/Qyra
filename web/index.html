Get Started in 4 Steps

How It Works

1

Install & Connect

Add the Qyra extension to Chrome and connect your QuickBooks Online account in one click. Everything is ready to scan in seconds.

2

Scan or Upload

Scan a POS report directly from the browser tab, upload an Excel file, or snap a photo of an invoice, check, or vendor credit.

3

Review & Map

AI extracts the data and auto-maps it to the right QuickBooks accounts. Review the preview and adjust mappings if needed.

4

Approve & Sync

For teams: draft entries go to managers for approval. Once approved, entries sync to QuickBooks automatically.

Built for Business Teams

Role-based access keeps your financial data secure and your workflow organized.

🏢

Admin

Full control over team members, pool resources, and permissions. Sets who can scan, draft, or sync directly to QuickBooks.

👔

Manager

Reviews and approves all draft entries from accountants and staff. Can modify entries before approving. Cannot change team permissions.

📊

Accountant

Scans documents and creates entries. Entries are saved as drafts for manager approval. Focused on data entry and accuracy.

🧑‍💻

Staff

Scans POS reports and uploads documents. Entries go through the approval workflow. Limited to scan and draft permissions.

Free and Starter plans include Admin and Staff roles. Professional plans and above unlock Manager and Accountant roles with approval workflows.
