Under the hood

Small extension. Serious bookkeeping engine.

Every feature exists because a bookkeeper asked for it. Here's what's waiting inside the popup.

AI that reads anything

AI OCR pulls structured data out of crumpled receipts, PDF invoices, and screenshots — then suggests the right fields with confidence scores.

5 transaction types

Journal Entries, Bills, Vendor Credits, Cheques, and Bill Payments — each with its own templates and defaults, all posting straight to QuickBooks Online.

Duplicate detection

Configurable dedup rules catch double-posts before they happen, with a force-sync override when you know better.

Template engine

Build templates from a live scan preview: map columns once, set default vendors, accounts, and departments, then reuse them on every scan.

Product intelligence

Fuzzy matching with contains, starts-with, exact, and regex rules. Unmatched products land in a review panel — nothing silently disappears.

Retry & full audit trail

Per-item sync results (synced, skipped, failed), one-click retries, bulk retry, and a complete sync log your accountant will love.

Roles & permissions

Owner and admin hierarchy, location-scoped access, invite-based onboarding, and email verification — security baked in, not bolted on.

Built for multi-location

Each admin manages their assigned locations. Owners see the whole operation. Everyone shares one clean books workflow.

Plays well with others
